-------------------

TypeScript/ES module source to a WeChat Mini Game-compatible CommonJS/ES5-style

8. Freesound special-piece recordings
-------------------------------------

The following adopted recordings are published under CC0 1.0 Universal:

- Firework Single Rocket #1, Kinoton:
  https://freesound.org/people/Kinoton/sounds/347163/
- Short Explosion.wav, animationIsaac:
  https://freesound.org/people/animationIsaac/sounds/207322/
- magical sound effect, reasanka:
  https://freesound.org/people/reasanka/sounds/429525/
- Cliche Magic Spell Sound, qubodup:
  https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/817466/

The first three recordings provide the independent rocket, bomb, and color-ball
one-shots. The fourth provides the single music-box cue used whenever one match
payload reports two or more triggered special pieces. Exact adopted source
files and SHA256 values are recorded in docs/design/sfx-acoustic-runtime-v8.md.

The samples in sections 4-8 are cropped, faded, filtered, gain-adjusted,
time-compressed where documented, layered, and
concatenated offline into res/audio/sfx-acoustic.m4a. The game ships only that
derived 22-cue AAC/M4A sprite, not the source archives. CC0 applies only to
these sounds and does not license the project as a whole.
