-------------------

Adopted commit:

The local adaptation is match3-wechat/js/core/grid.js. It changes the
TypeScript/ES module source to a WeChat Mini Game-compatible CommonJS/ES5-style
module and adds Chinese comments. It adopts board generation, random type
